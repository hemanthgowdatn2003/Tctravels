OUR STORY & HERITAGE

Born in the Royal City of Mysuru

Located right near the historic Mysore Palace at Gandhi Square, TC Travels Mysore was founded with a singular mission: to make travel completely transparent, comfortable, and memorable for families, tourists, and business travelers.

We recognized that travelers often face hidden charges, unverified drivers, and unclear kilometer calculations. At TC Travels, we pioneered clear Per-KM pricing with standard mandatory daily KM limits, so customers always know what they are paying for.

Additionally, we introduced our highly-rated Personal Acting Driver service, enabling car owners in Mysuru to sit back and enjoy their own vehicles while our verified chauffeurs handle city traffic and curvy ghat roads.
